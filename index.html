Fly.ai integration

The browser worker now consumes fly.ai's committed web export: compressed connectome weights plus neuron metadata. Real fly cell types such as LC4, LPLC2, LPLC1 and LC10a are used as sensory entry points, while descending/motor populations provide the behaviour readout. The renderer remains local and does not require Three.js or another external CDN.
